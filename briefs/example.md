# example: Why is the sky blue?

> The kit's demo video. It exists to show every pipeline step working end to end
> (`bun run preview` shows 12/12), not to be a strong short. It runs offline on a Mac
> with the built-in `say` voice.

## The idea in one sentence

After watching, the viewer can say: "Air scatters blue light more than the other
colours, so blue comes at me from the whole sky."

The question the hook asks (and the payoff answers): sunlight looks white, so why is
the sky blue and not white?

## Flow

- **Chosen:** H, Explain it. The fact is a mechanism with one hard step (scattering),
  so the video climbs a short ladder of questions: what is sunlight made of, how do
  colours differ, what is in the air, what does the air do to blue.
- **Rejected shape:** A, Three reasons. There are not three independent reasons, only
  one mechanism in layers; numbering them would fake a structure the facts do not have.
- What replaces spoken numbering: the order of questions, each scene answering the one
  the previous scene raised.
- The moment (a person, a place, a time, an action: §3c rule 6): you, looking up at a
  clear sky (f-0003). A demo topic has no historical moment; a real video should.

One held world for the whole video (`World.tsx`): sky, sun, clouds and sea from
`src/shared/nature`, with information added on the same image instead of cutting to
new images (§5g).
